        inputBufferBytes!: Int64 = 4096, maximumReadBytes!: Int64 = 16 * 1024)

Checks positive policy values against fixed hard ceilings\.

#### 参数

- `maximumEncodedBytes` — Input cap, at most 1 GiB\.
- `maximumDecodedBytes` — Output cap, at most 1 GiB\.
- `maximumHeaderBytes` — Header scan cap, at most 64 KiB\.
- `maximumMembers` — Member count, at most 128\.
- `maximumBlocks` — Block count, at most 65536\.
- `inputBufferBytes` — Input buffer size, at most 64 KiB\.
- `maximumReadBytes` — Per\-read output, at most 64 KiB\.
