wirestack.ExternalDecryptor

interfacepublicsemantic: partial src/tls_contract.cj:209:1

签名
public interface ExternalDecryptor
⧉
概览

Caller-owned, thread-safe service for an external RSA private operation.

The TLS engine passes the exact handshake OperationContext. A successful callback transfers ownership of its returned array to Wirestack: it must contain exactly request.ciphertextBytes().size raw-RSA block bytes and the callback must not retain, reuse, or mutate it. Wirestack zeroes that array after native completion on every path.
